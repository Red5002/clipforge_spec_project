Common Questions

Clear Reassurance

"Do I really need another editing tool?"

You don't need another complicated timeline editor. ClipForge isn't designed to replace complex post-production software—it is built specifically around finding useful moments faster.

"What if my video is long?"

That is exactly what ClipForge is built for: starting with long-form content (like podcasts or long tutorials) and helping you identify shorter moments worth turning into clips.

"Will I still have control over the final output?"

Yes. ClipForge assists your process rather than completely replacing your creative judgment. It highlights potential moments; you make the final decision on what content is worth publishing.
